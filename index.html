Account Deletion Request

If you'd like to delete your account and associated data from Persist - Daily Prayer, please follow the instructions below.

How to Request Account Deletion

To delete your account and associated data:

  1. Send an email to info@apps29.dev with the subject line "Account Deletion Request"
  2. Include the following information in your email:
    • Your account email address or username
    • The device you used (optional, helps us locate your data)

Note: For security reasons, we may need to verify your identity before processing the deletion request.

What Happens When You Delete Your Account

When you request account deletion:

  • Deleted immediately:
    • Your profile information
    • Your personal settings
    • All user-generated content
  • Retained for legal/compliance purposes (30 days):
    • Transaction records
    • Customer support tickets

After this period, all data will be permanently erased from our systems.

Processing Time

We process deletion requests within 7 business days of verification.

You'll receive a confirmation email when your account and data have been deleted.

If you have any questions, please contact us at info@apps29.dev.
